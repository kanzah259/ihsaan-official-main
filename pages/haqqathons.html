What is a hackathon?

A hackathon is a focused sprint where teams turn real-world problems into working ideas. Over a weekend, participants form teams, learn from mentors, prototype a solution, and pitch it to partners who can help take it further.

"The hackathon was an incredible experience. I learned so much from the people around me, got hands on experience with the latest AI tools and loved seeing participants build solutions to real world problems."
