Privacy notice

How IHSAAN handles personal information on this website.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information is collected through ihsaan.network, why it is used, and the choices available to you. It covers the public marketing site and the opportunities tracker on this domain. It does not cover third-party sites we link to, such as the member portal or external forms, which have their own privacy information.

Who is responsible

IHSAAN operates this website. For privacy questions, contact ihsaan@fosis.org.uk.

The precise registered legal entity that acts as controller under UK data protection law (for example whether that is IHSAAN under a specific corporate name, or another body such as FOSIS) is subject to owner confirmation. This notice will be updated when that identity is confirmed.

What we collect and why

Contact messages

The home-page contact form asks for your name, email address, and message. Submitting the form opens your email application so you can send that message to ihsaan@fosis.org.uk. We use the information you send to respond to your enquiry. You can also email us directly without using the form.

Opportunities tracker accounts

Contributor sign-in and password recovery collect email addresses and passwords (passwords are handled by our authentication provider; we do not store them in plain text on this site). Administrators may create contributor accounts with an email address, role, and starting password. Tracker use may also store opportunity content and related timestamps or authorship references. This information is used to run the tracker, control access, and maintain the opportunity listings.

Analytics

This site loads Google Analytics (measurement ID G-7BTG3GEDNT). Analytics may collect device and usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, and identifiers used to measure site traffic. How this is configured, and the lawful basis relied on for your audience, is subject to owner review (see related compliance work on analytics choice and objection controls).

Technical and delivery data

Like most websites, hosting and delivery infrastructure may process IP addresses and standard server or network logs needed to serve pages securely and reliably.

Who we share information with

Depending on how you use the site, personal information may be processed by:

  • Supabase — authentication and database services for the opportunities tracker
  • Google — Analytics on this site; also Google Forms if you follow an external signup link
  • Email providers — when you contact us by email or via the mailto contact form
  • Embedded or linked services — for example calendar embeds or Notion pages you choose to open
  • Member portal — portal.ihsaan.network is a separate service with its own privacy information

We do not sell your personal information.

International transfers

Some providers above may process information outside the UK. Where that happens, it is under the provider’s transfer mechanisms and contracts. Ask us if you need more detail for a specific service.

Retention

Contact emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any related follow-up. Tracker accounts and opportunity records are kept while the account or listing remains useful for the programme, or until access is removed or a deletion request is completed. Analytics retention follows the Google Analytics property settings. Formal retention schedules are subject to owner documentation.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). To exercise rights related to this site, email ihsaan@fosis.org.uk. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Children

This site is aimed at university students, graduates, and professional partners. It is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this notice when our processing or legal details change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Privacy enquiries: ihsaan@fosis.org.uk
