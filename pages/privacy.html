Privacy notice

How IHSAAN handles personal information on this website.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This notice explains what personal information is collected through ihsaan.network, why it is used, the legal bases we rely on, and the choices available to you under UK data protection law (including UK GDPR). It covers the public marketing site and the opportunities tracker on this domain. It does not cover third-party sites we link to, such as the member portal or external forms, which have their own privacy information.

Who is responsible

IHSAAN operates this website and is responsible for the personal information described here (the “controller” once the registered legal entity is confirmed). For privacy questions, contact ihsaan@fosis.org.uk.

The precise registered legal entity that acts as controller under UK data protection law (for example whether that is IHSAAN under a specific corporate name, or another body such as FOSIS) is subject to owner confirmation. This notice will be updated when that identity is confirmed.

Personal information we collect

Information you provide

  • Contact data — name, email address, and message content when you use the home-page contact form or email us at ihsaan@fosis.org.uk. Submitting the form opens your email application so you can send the message.
  • Account data — contributor email address and password for opportunities-tracker sign-in and recovery (passwords are handled by our authentication provider; we do not store them in plain text on this site).
  • Tracker content — opportunity listings and related fields that contributors submit, which may include timestamps or authorship references.
  • Admin-created account details — when an administrator adds a contributor, email address, role, and a starting password may be stored to set up access.

Information collected automatically

  • Analytics data — this site loads Google Analytics (measurement ID G-7BTG3GEDNT), which may collect device and usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP address, and identifiers used to measure site traffic.
  • Technical and delivery data — hosting and delivery infrastructure may process IP addresses and standard server or network logs needed to serve pages securely and reliably.

How we use personal information

  • Respond to enquiries and partnership or programme messages
  • Provide and secure opportunities-tracker accounts and listings
  • Understand how the site is used and improve content and reliability
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of the site and related systems
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests

Legal bases

Under UK GDPR, we need a lawful basis for each use of personal information. The table below reflects how this site is intended to operate. Analytics basis and any consent or objection controls remain subject to owner review.

Purpose Typical information Legal basis
Responding to contact messages Name, email, message Legitimate interests — handling enquiries about IHSAAN programmes and partnerships; or steps toward a contract where relevant
Opportunities tracker accounts and submissions Email, credentials, role, opportunity content Legitimate interests — running the contributor tracker; contractual necessity where access is provided under programme arrangements
Site security and delivery IP address, technical logs Legitimate interests — keeping the site secure and available; compliance with law where applicable
Analytics and site improvement Device and usage data via Google Analytics Subject to owner confirmation — may rely on legitimate interests where a qualifying UK analytics approach applies, or on consent where required. Choice and objection controls are tracked separately
Legal compliance and protection Contact and account data as needed Compliance with law; legitimate interests — establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims and protecting rights and safety

Who we share information with

Depending on how you use the site, personal information may be processed by:

  • Supabase — authentication and database services for the opportunities tracker
  • Google — Analytics on this site; also Google Forms if you follow an external signup link
  • Email providers — when you contact us by email or via the mailto contact form
  • Embedded or linked services — for example calendar embeds or Notion pages you choose to open
  • Member portal — portal.ihsaan.network is a separate service with its own privacy information

We do not sell your personal information.

International transfers

Some providers above may process personal information outside the UK (for example in the European Economic Area or the United States). Where information is transferred to a country that is not covered by a UK adequacy decision, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the provider’s standard contractual clauses or other approved transfer mechanisms, or on another lawful transfer ground where applicable. Contact us if you need more detail about a specific service.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, including follow-up on enquiries, running the tracker, security, and legal requirements.

  • Contact emails — as long as needed to handle your enquiry and related follow-up
  • Tracker accounts and opportunity records — while the account or listing remains useful for the programme, or until access is removed or a deletion request is completed
  • Analytics — according to the Google Analytics property settings

Formal retention schedules and erasure workflows are subject to owner documentation.

Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the information we process (including authenticated access controls for the tracker). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may ask us to take the following actions in relation to personal information we hold about you:

  • Access — information about our processing, and a copy of your personal information
  • Correct — update or correct inaccurate personal information
  • Delete — erase personal information where we no longer have a good reason to keep it
  • Restrict — limit how we use your personal information in certain cases
  • Object — object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to processing for direct marketing
  • Portability — receive a machine-readable copy of personal information you provided to us, where applicable
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time (this does not affect earlier processing)

To exercise these rights, email ihsaan@fosis.org.uk. We may need to verify your identity. Whether we must fulfil a request depends on the legal basis and context; if we refuse a request in whole or in part, we will explain why where we are allowed to do so.

You also have the right to complain to the UK regulator, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO):

  • Website: ico.org.uk
  • Address: Information Commissioner’s Office, Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF
  • Telephone: +44 303 123 1113

Automated decision-making

We do not use personal information from this site for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects about you.

Children

This site is aimed at university students, graduates, and professional partners. It is not intended for children under 13. If you believe we have collected personal information from a child in a way that is not allowed, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

Changes

We may update this notice when our processing or legal details change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
