A Common Mistake

Ever wonder why so many graduates spend months applying and facing rejections before securing a job?

One of the key reasons is that many students mistakenly wait until their final year to explore career options, build a CV, gain essential skills, and apply for jobs.

This delay leads to:

  • Missed opportunities
  • Lack of application practice
  • No industry experience

On the other hand, students who ”Start Early” gain major advantages over everyone else.

Three Advantages of Students Who Start Early

  1. More likely to have gained experience in industry and obtained key skills
  2. More likely to know which specific field of work they are interested in
  3. Much more familiar with the job application process

Practical Steps: Example Timeline for a 3-Year Degree

First Year

  • Start researching jobs in industries you're interested in
  • Check for Spring Weeks and Insight Days
  • Create a basic CV and set up a LinkedIn profile
  • Apply to Spring Weeks and Insight Days (especially Sep–Jan)

Second Year

  • Attend career fairs and networking events
  • Refine CV and LinkedIn; consider finding a mentor
  • Practice with mock interviews and online assessments
  • Apply for summer internships (especially Aug–Mar)

Third Year

  • Refine everything: CVs, Cover Letters, LinkedIn, Network, Interview Skills, Assessment Practice
  • Attend graduate career fairs and explore job websites
  • Apply for graduate roles and schemes (especially Aug–Mar)
